# Website Opening Intro Videos Folder

Place your opening intro MP4 videos in this folder. These videos are played full screen before the actual website is opened.

### Required Video Files:

1. **`intro-landscape.mp4`** (or `landscape.mp4`):
   - Played when the website is opened on Landscape screens (desktop monitors, laptops, tablets in landscape).
   - Stretches to the full screen.

2. **`intro-portrait.mp4`** (or `portrait.mp4`):
   - Played when the website is opened on Portrait screens (mobile phones, portrait orientation).
   - Stretches to the full screen.

### Features & Behavior:
- **Full-Screen Stretched**: The video automatically stretches to fill the entire screen (`w-full h-full object-fill`) regardless of aspect ratio or display dimensions.
- **Autoplay & Audio**: Starts playback automatically upon page load.
- **Auto-Enter**: Once the video finishes playing, the intro seamlessly transitions and opens the museum website.
- **Skip Button**: Visitors can click "Skip Intro" in the top corner at any time to enter the museum immediately.
